Who we are

Built at the intersection of business and technology

We believe the best decisions happen when strategic thinking and technical depth sit at the same table.

SearchingBinary was founded by two partners with deeply complementary skills. One brings decades of experience in business analysis, requirements engineering, and quality assurance.
