We founded SearchingBinary on a simple belief: the best business decisions happen when strategic thinking and technical depth sit at the same table.

Our partnership brings together two deeply complementary perspectives. One of us has spent decades in business analysis, requirements engineering, and quality assurance, bringing structure and rigor to every engagement. The other brings technical leadership across AI, cloud, product, and engineering, with experience shared across teams and conference stages worldwide. Together, we cover the full spectrum from strategy to execution.

## What we do

We work across three disciplines:

- **Investment**: strategic capital allocation for technology-forward companies
- **Business Advisory**: from requirements engineering to go-to-market strategy, we bring the rigor of structured analysis to every business decision
- **Technology Advisory**: deep expertise across AI, cloud, product, UX, and engineering leadership

Each practice informs the others. Investment insight sharpens advisory. Decades of combined experience across business and technology ground strategy in reality.

## Our approach

We take on fewer engagements to go deeper. Every client gets senior attention, not a junior team with a playbook. We measure success by what changes in your business, not by the weight of our slide decks.
