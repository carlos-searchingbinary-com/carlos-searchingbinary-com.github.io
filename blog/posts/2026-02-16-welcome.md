We founded SearchingBinary on a simple belief: the best business decisions happen when strategic thinking and technical depth sit at the same table.

After 21 years of building, scaling, and advising across AI, cloud, product, UX, and engineering, we decided to bring that experience together under one roof.

## What we do

We work across three disciplines:

- **Investment** - strategic capital allocation for technology-forward companies
- **Business Advisory** - clarity for companies at inflection points
- **Technology Advisory** - senior guidance across AI, cloud, product, UX, and engineering

Each practice informs the others. Investment insight sharpens advisory. Two decades of technical depth grounds strategy in reality.

## Our approach

We take on fewer engagements to go deeper. Every client gets senior attention, not a junior team with a playbook. We measure success by what changes in your business, not by the weight of our slide decks.

If you're navigating a strategic decision or building your technical capabilities, [get in touch](mailto:hello@searchingbinary.com).
